# Pinoy Online Venture — client guide

This app is your community website. Visitors can browse members, events, and videos, then message you on Messenger. You manage the content from a private admin area — no coding needed.

Website: [https://pinoyonlineventures.com](https://pinoyonlineventures.com)

Admin login: [https://pinoyonlineventures.com/admin/login](https://pinoyonlineventures.com/admin/login)

---

## What the website does

**Home**  
The first screen visitors see. It shows your message, About story, mission, vision, community numbers, featured members, events, videos, and a button to message you.

**Community**  
A directory of your people. Visitors can search by name, role, or company, filter by category, and open a full profile.

**Founders**  
Introduces the people behind Pinoy Online Venture, including their photo, role, story, and contact links.

**Events**  
Your gatherings plus the video library. Each event can have a photo, date, place, map link, and a button to message you about it. Videos play on the same page.

**News**  
Announcements, achievements, event updates, and community stories. Each article can contain an image or a video and full details.

**Partnership**  
A coming-soon page for future partner opportunities. Content will be managed from admin later.

**Message buttons**  
Orange buttons that open Facebook Messenger so people can talk to you.

**Add to phone**  
On a phone, visitors can add the site to their home screen so it opens like an app.

## How visitors use it

### Move around

The bar at the bottom has seven buttons: Home, Community, Founders, Events, News, Partnership, and Login.

The menu (☰) at the top right opens extra links, including your social pages.

On a member or event page, the back arrow returns to the list.

### Find a member

1. Tap **Community**.
2. Type a name, role, or company in the search box.
3. Tap a category chip to narrow the list (chips appear after you add categories in admin).
4. Tap a card to open the profile.

### Watch a video

1. Tap **Events**, or tap **Video Library** on Home.
2. Scroll to **Video library**.
3. Tap the orange play button. The video plays on the same page.

If a video has **Message us**, that button is for chatting with you — not for opening YouTube.

### Contact you

Tap any **Message** button. Messenger opens so they can send you a chat.

### Put the app on a phone

1. Open the website in the phone browser.
2. Choose **Add to Home Screen** (or **Install app**).
3. Open the new icon. It looks and feels like a regular app.

### Open other screens

1. Tap the **menu (☰)** at the top right.
2. Choose the page you want (Homepage, Members, Events, and so on).
3. **View public app** opens the live website so you can check your changes.
4. **Sign out** when you are done.

### The same save steps on every screen

1. Tap **Add**.
2. Fill in the form. Fields with * must be completed.
3. For photos: tap the upload box or drag a picture in. Use JPG, PNG, or WEBP.
4. Tap **Save changes**.
5. To change something later, tap the pencil. To remove it, tap the trash can and confirm.

**What the switches mean**

- **Visible / Active / Published** — visitors can see it. If this is off, only you see it in admin.
- **Featured** — extra spotlight (for example, a member on Home, or a video at the top of Videos).
- **Display order** — smaller number appears first.

After you save, open the public website and check that it looks right.

### Page links (slugs)

When you type a name or title, the app creates a short web address, like `/community/juan-dela-cruz`.

You can edit it before you save. After you have already shared that link, do not change it, or the old link will stop working.

## Admin screens, one by one

### Homepage

Menu → **Homepage**

Three blocks:

1. **Hero** — big headline, short message, and the main orange button.
2. **About preview** — photo and introduction shown on Home.
3. **Final call to action** — orange box near the bottom with a message button.

Turn **Visible** on for each block you want people to see.

The **Video Library** button on Home always goes to the video section on Events. You do not edit that button here.

---

### Categories

Menu → **Categories**

Create groups first (for example Mentor, Leader, Partner, Entrepreneur).

Then assign them when you add members. Active categories show as filter chips on Community.

---

### Members

Menu → **Members**

1. Add categories first, if you use them.
2. Tap **Add member**.
3. Enter name, photo, role, short intro, full bio, company, location, and social / Messenger links.
4. Turn on **Visible on public site**.
5. Turn on **Featured member** if they should appear on Home.
6. Save.

---

### Founders

Menu → **Founders**

1. Tap **Add founder**.
2. Enter the founder's name, role, photo, short introduction, and full story.
3. Add optional Messenger, Facebook, or website links.
4. Use **Display order** to choose who appears first.
5. Keep **Visible on public site** on.
6. Save.

Visitors can open **Founders** from the main website menu.

---

### Events

Menu → **Events**

1. Tap **Add event**.
2. Enter a title and start date (required).
3. Add a cover photo, short summary, full description, location, and map link if you have one.
4. Add a button label and Messenger link if people should inquire.
5. Turn on **Published**.
6. Save.

The app usually marks events as upcoming, ongoing, or completed from the dates. Only use **Status override** if you need to force a different label.

---

### Videos

Menu → **Videos**

1. Tap **Add video**.
2. Paste the YouTube link (regular watch link, share link, or Shorts).
3. Add a title. Description is optional.
4. You can upload your own thumbnail if you do not like the YouTube picture.
5. Optional: button label + Messenger link so viewers can message you after watching.
6. Turn on **Published**.
7. Turn on **Featured video** for the one video that should sit at the top of the Events video library.
8. Save.

Visitors watch on Events. Do not put a YouTube link in the Messenger button field.

---

### Partnership

Partnership is a public Coming Soon page for now. It is not editable in admin yet.

---

### News

Menu → **News**

1. Tap **Add article**.
2. Enter the headline and choose a category.
3. Add a short summary and the full article details.
4. Upload an article image **or** paste a YouTube video link. At least one is required.
5. Set the publication date.
6. Turn on **Featured article** to place it at the top of News.
7. Turn on **Published**, then save.

Visitors can filter news by category and open each item to read the full article or play its video.

---

### Gallery

Menu → **Gallery**

Upload community photos for Home.

Give each photo a title or caption if you want a label on the picture. Use **Display order** to choose which photos show first.

---

### Media library

Menu → **Media library**

A place to upload extra pictures and copy their links. You can usually skip this and upload photos **directly** on Members, Events, Videos, or Gallery.

---

### Social links

Menu → **Social links**

These are the Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube icons in the footer and menu.

Edit the label and the URL. Turn **Visible** off to hide a link without deleting it.

---

### CTA / Messenger

Menu → **CTA / Messenger**

This is where you store your Messenger chat links and button labels used around the site.

Use a full link that starts with `https://`.

---

### About content

Menu → **About content**

These three pieces now appear as sections on the Home page:

- **About story** — main story and optional photo
- **Mission**
- **Vision**

## Quick checks

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| You can sign in but it says you need administrator access | Ask the person who set up the site to give your email admin access |
| A member does not show on Community | Turn on **Visible on public site** and save |
| An event or video does not show | Turn on **Published** and save |
| A member does not show on Home | Turn on **Featured member** (and keep them visible) |
| Message button is missing | Add a button label and a Messenger link that starts with `https://` |
| Photo will not upload | Use a JPG, PNG, or WEBP picture |
| YouTube link is not accepted | Paste the full YouTube link from the share button |
| Facebook still shows an old picture when you share the site | Ask your developer to refresh the Facebook preview, or wait and share the link again after a few minutes |

If you need a new admin account, another login, or a password reset, contact the person who set up the website for you.
